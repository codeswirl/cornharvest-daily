Privacy Policy

Corn Harvest · Last updated 14 October 2026

Corn Harvest has no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no account you need to create with us. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Game progress is stored on your device. Apple services and downloads of daily puzzles involve the limited network activity described below.

What the app stores on your device

  • Game progress: completed levels, stars, best times, unlocked fields, corn balance, and tutorial status.
  • Daily puzzle progress: solve times, streaks, attempts, and any score waiting to be submitted to Game Center.
  • Preferences: music, sound, and haptic settings.
  • Purchase status: a cached indication of whether the one-time unlock is owned.
  • Downloaded puzzles: a local cache so a downloaded daily puzzle can be played without downloading it again.

The app does not upload this local progress to a developer-operated account or database. A daily solve time may be sent to Apple for the leaderboard as described below. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple device and backup settings.

In-app purchases

The optional full-game unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and purchase records. Corn Harvest uses StoreKit to verify ownership and restore purchases; we do not receive your payment card details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple's handling of purchase information.

Game Center and the daily leaderboard

You can play without signing in to Game Center. If you are signed in and own the full-game unlock, the app submits an eligible daily solve time to Apple's Game Center leaderboard. A solve recorded before purchase may be submitted after you unlock the leaderboard on the same puzzle day.

Game Center associates scores with your Game Center player identity. Your nickname, score, and rank can be visible to other players. To display the leaderboard and identify your own entry, the app reads player display names, game-specific player identifiers, scores, and ranks from Apple. The app does not forward that information to a separate developer server.

Apple manages Game Center accounts and data. You can manage Game Center sign-in and privacy settings in your device's Settings. See Game Center & Privacy for details.

Daily puzzle downloads and this website

Campaign levels and bonus fields are bundled in the app. Daily puzzles are downloaded over HTTPS from a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. These requests retrieve a puzzle file for a date; the app does not attach your name, Game Center identifier, purchase status, or game progress to them.

As with other internet connections, the host receives network information such as your IP address. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This also applies when you visit this privacy-policy page. GitHub's handling and retention of hosting data are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies to this page.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy questions. Please do not send payment details, passwords, or other sensitive information. We keep correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations; you can contact us to request deletion.

Your choices and deletion

Settings → Reset Progress clears your local game progress and resets game preferences. It does not revoke your purchase or delete scores already submitted to Game Center. Deleting the app removes its local app data from that device, though copies may remain in device backups. Apple purchase records, Game Center information, and GitHub hosting logs are managed by their respective providers; resetting or deleting Corn Harvest does not erase those records.

Children

The game does not ask players for their name, email address, age, or other personal details, and it has no chat or advertising features. Apple purchases, Game Center, and GitHub hosting operate as described above. Parents and guardians can use Apple's device restrictions to manage purchases and Game Center access. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through a support request, contact us so we can address it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above.
