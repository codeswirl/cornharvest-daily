Corn Harvest Support

Help for players on iPhone and iPad

Need help with a puzzle, a purchase, or something that isn't working? Email info@fivekudos.com.

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the app version shown in Settings, and what happened. If helpful, include the level number or a screenshot with personal information removed. Never send passwords or payment card details.

Restore your purchase

In Corn Harvest, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. You can also restore from the “Unlock Every Puzzle!” purchase screen. Use the Apple Account that originally bought the unlock and make sure you have an internet connection.

The unlock is a one-time purchase for levels 16–100 and the Daily Puzzle leaderboard. It does not skip level progression. If restoration does not work, contact us at the email address above.

Find the Daily Puzzle and bonus fields

Complete level 15 to open the Daily Puzzle on the main menu and the bonus fields. Daily puzzles are free to play; the leaderboard requires the full-game unlock and Game Center sign-in.

A new daily puzzle starts at 00:00 UTC. Internet access is needed to download it. If today's puzzle is unavailable, check your connection and reopen the app. Campaign levels and bonus fields can be played offline.

Daily leaderboard help

Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings and confirm that the full-game unlock is active. The first eligible solve time is used for that day's leaderboard. If a score fails to send, reconnect and return to the main menu on the same UTC day so the app can retry. An expired day's score cannot be posted to the new day's competition.

Learn to play

Open Settings → How to Play to replay the tutorial. Drag the tractor through the numbered barns in order, harvest every plot exactly once, and finish at the last barn. You can retrace your route, undo, or restart if you take a wrong turn.

Progress and settings

Progress is stored locally on your device. Settings → Reset Progress erases game progress and resets preferences, but does not revoke your purchase. Deleting the app can remove your local progress, so contact us before reinstalling if you want help troubleshooting.
